Reflection
Hardest concept

The hardest part of this course was not one single concept but the pace. I started from scratch and worked mostly with AI tools, and I did not always get enough time to be taught each idea properly. I was always on my toes, worried I might miss a deadline before I fully understood the topic. I got through it by breaking big topics into small steps, learning by building things, and asking questions whenever I got stuck. Along the way, I also got a real feel for how software is made and what kind of work software companies do every day.

Capstone improvement

Based on the feedback I received, I would improve my capstone by adding more explanations. My project works, but a reader may not understand why I made certain choices. I would explain each part in simple language, describe how the pieces connect, and add short notes about the decisions and trade-offs I made, so anyone can follow my thinking.

What I'll learn next

Next, I want to learn data analytics. I did some data work in the past, so it already feels familiar, and I enjoy it. I plan to learn how to clean data, analyze it, and turn it into charts and insights that help people make decisions. I also want to connect it with what I learned here, like databases and APIs, because data and software work closely together.
